**Store:** [app/2543340](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2543340/Only_go_up/)

You keep climbing **up** to escape rising water — a 3D platform / parkour-style climb.

## 2. THE LAST BREATH

**Store:** [app/2442490](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2442490/THE_LAST_BREATH/)

### Overview

**2051** — a world broken by air pollution.  
Farm resources, avoid looters, survive a post-apocalyptic sandbox.

Store blurb:

> Survive the post-apocalypse world. … Online and local party games enjoyed by 1 to 5 players.

### Genre · tags (store)

- Genres: Action · Adventure · Indie · Strategy  
- Tags e.g. Survival · Open World · Survival Horror · Multiplayer · Co-op · Crafting · Base Building · Post-apocalyptic  

### Play features (About This Game)

- Resource farming · avoid raiders · **gun / sword combat**  
- **Solo** or **1–5 player** online / local party  
- Features: Online/LAN PvP · Online/LAN Co-op · Shared/Split Screen · Steam Achievements (10) · Remote Play Together  
- Controls e.g. WASD · E inventory/status · F interact · ESC pause  

### Specs · price (store)
